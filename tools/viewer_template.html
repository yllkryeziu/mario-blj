Backwards Long Jump Replay

One scripted input program, four floors. Walk away from the rise, crouch, long jump, then hold the stick back and re-press A on every landing. Whether Mario's speed runs away or stalls is decided entirely by the shape of the ground under him. On the real endless staircase the instant warp keeps throwing him back down, marked as rings, until one jump crosses the whole trigger zone inside a single frame.

Every long jump multiplies forwardVel by 1.5 and the clamp that follows it only catches positive speed, so a negative value grows without bound. Air drag pushes it back toward −16 at roughly 0.85 per frame, which means the chain only survives if the air phase is short. Flat ground and ramps give 20 to 30 air frames and the chain stalls near −22. Stair treads are level however steep the staircase is, so they give one or two air frames and the same input program runs away.
