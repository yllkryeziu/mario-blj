Each long jump multiplies forwardVel by 1.5 and the clamp that follows only catches positive speed, so a negative value grows without bound. Air drag pulls it back toward the −16 attractor at about 0.85 per frame, which means the chain only survives when the air phase is short. Stair treads are level however steep the staircase is, so they give one or two air frames and the chain runs away. The red triangles are the instant warp: once a frame, if Mario's floor is one of them, the level script displaces him 205 down and 410 back down-stream. The zone is only 154 units deep in z and the long jump clamp is 48, so the only way past it is a speed no ordinary action can reach.
